Privacy Policy for Arrow Puzzle

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Arrow Puzzle" mobile game ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Puzzle does not ask for or collect personal information such as your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. No account or sign-in is needed to play.

The only information that leaves your device is what is needed for the Global Leaderboard:

  • A leaderboard nickname — generated automatically by the App (for example "SwiftFalcon42"). You cannot type your own name, so it never contains personal details. You can change it at any time with the "🎲 New name" button in Settings.
  • Your total stars and number of levels cleared, and the time of your last update.
  • A random anonymous player ID, created by Google Firebase Anonymous Authentication so that only your device can update your own leaderboard entry. It is not linked to your Google account, phone number, or device identifiers.

Leaderboard entries (nickname and stars) are visible to other players of the App. This information is used only to show the leaderboard. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for tracking.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Level progress and star ratings
  • Daily challenge results and streaks
  • Hint count
  • Settings (sound, theme, hint button) and your leaderboard nickname

This data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the App.

3. Deleting Your Data

Use Settings → Reset All Progress in the App. This deletes your progress on the device and permanently deletes your leaderboard entry from our server. You can also email us (address below) and we will delete it for you.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services — required for installing and updating the App. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Anonymous Authentication) — stores the leaderboard. Data is encrypted in transit. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the App (a banner and optional "watch an ad for hints" videos). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), and app interaction and diagnostic data to serve, measure, and prevent fraud on ads. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the AdMob privacy page.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Leaderboard nicknames are generated by the App and cannot be typed, there is no chat, and no account is required. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

6. Permissions

The App uses the Internet permission (for the leaderboard and ads) and the Advertising ID permission (used by Google AdMob). The game itself works fully offline.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
